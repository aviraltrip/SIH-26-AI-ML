OFFICIAL SCHEME POLICY GUIDELINES & FAQS (SOURCE OF TRUTH)
================================================================================

1. MAHILA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA
- Description: A micro-finance scheme targeting women entrepreneurs belonging to Backward Classes to start or expand small businesses.
- Loan Limit: Maximum loan amount is ₹1,40,000.
- Coverage: Up to 95% of the total project cost is covered by the scheme. The beneficiary contribution is 5%.
- Interest Rate: 4% per annum interest rate.
- Target Beneficiaries: Women entrepreneurs from Backward Classes (as defined by government lists) living below or near the poverty line.
- Typical Trades: Tailoring, boutique, small grocery, dairy, beauty parlor, handicrafts.
- Moratorium Period: A repayment holiday of up to 3 to 6 months depending on the trade and bank discretion.

2. MICRO CREDIT SCHEME
- Description: Micro-finance credit for individual self-employment projects for persons belonging to Backward Classes.
- Loan Limit: Maximum loan amount is ₹1,50,000.
- Coverage: Up to 90% of the project cost. The beneficiary contribution is 10%.
- Interest Rate: 6% per annum interest rate.
- Target Beneficiaries: Entrepreneurs belonging to Backward Classes (both male and female) whose family income is within eligibility limits.
- Typical Trades: Small shops, local trading, repair services, carpentry, weaving.

3. PM VISHWAKARMA SCHEME
- Description: Central government scheme to support traditional artisans and craftspeople who work with their hands and tools.
- Benefits:
  - Collateral-free enterprise development loans of up to ₹3,00,000.
  - The loan is provided in two tranches: Tranche 1 of up to ₹1,00,000 with a repayment period of 18 months, and Tranche 2 of up to ₹2,00,000 with a repayment period of 30 months.
  - Concessional Interest Rate: 5% per annum.
  - Skill Training: Basic training (5-7 days) and Advanced training (15 days or more) with a stipend of ₹500 per day.
  - Toolkit Incentive: A grant/incentive of ₹15,000 for purchasing tools.
  - Marketing Support: Assistance in quality certification, branding, and e-commerce onboarding.
- Eligible Trades: 18 traditional trades are covered, including:
  1. Tailor (Darzi)
  2. Barber (Naayi)
  3. Welder
  4. Carpenter (Suthar/Badhai)
  5. Blacksmith (Lohar)
  6. Potter (Kumhaar)
  7. Cobbler (Charmakar/Shoemaker)
  8. Mason (Rajmistri)
  9. Basket/Mat/Broom Maker
  10. Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional)
  11. Sculptor (Murtiakar, Stone carver)
  12. Goldsmith (Sunar)
  13. Locksmith
  14. Armor maker
  15. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
  16. Stone breaker
  17. Boat Maker
  18. Fishing Net Maker
- Eligibility Criteria:
  - An artisan or craftsperson working in one of the 18 family-based traditional trades.
  - Minimum age of 18 years on the date of registration.
  - The benefit is restricted to one member of a family (family defined as husband, wife, and unmarried children).
  - Persons in government service and their family members are not eligible.

4. PRIME MINISTER'S EMPLOYMENT GENERATION PROGRAMME (PMEGP)
- Description: A credit-linked subsidy program for setting up new micro-enterprises in manufacturing, services, or trading sectors.
- Maximum Project Cost:
  - Manufacturing Sector: Up to ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs).
  - Service or Trading Sector: Up to ₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakhs).
- Subsidy Rates (Margin Money):
  - General Category: 15% subsidy in Urban areas, 25% subsidy in Rural areas. Beneficiary contribution is 10% of project cost.
  - Special Category (SC, ST, OBC, Minorities, Women, Ex-servicemen, Physically Handicapped, NER, Hill and Border areas): 25% subsidy in Urban areas, 35% subsidy in Rural areas. Beneficiary contribution is 5% of project cost.
- Funding Partner: Public sector banks, regional rural banks, and cooperative banks.
- Loan Details: The bank sanctions a term loan for the project. The interest rate is at normal bank lending rates.
- Eligibility Criteria:
  - Any individual above 18 years of age.
  - For projects costing above ₹10 Lakhs in manufacturing and above ₹5 Lakhs in service/trading, the beneficiary must have passed at least VIII standard (8th grade).
  - Only new projects are eligible for assistance under PMEGP. Existing units or units already receiving subsidy under other schemes are not eligible.
  - Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and Cooperative Societies are also eligible.
