OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SCHEME POLICY GUIDELINES & FAQS (SOURCE OF TRUTH)

1. MAHILA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA (MSY) - NBCFDC
- Description: A micro-finance scheme implemented by the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (NBCFDC) to provide credit assistance to women entrepreneurs belonging to Backward Classes to start or expand small businesses.
- Loan Limits:
  * Maximum loan limit per individual beneficiary is ₹1,25,000 (the local matching portal supports up to ₹1,40,000).
  * Maximum loan limit per Self-Help Group (SHG) is ₹15,00,000.
- Pattern of Financing: NBCFDC provides loan assistance of up to 95% of the project cost. The remaining 5% is contributed by the State Channelizing Agency (SCA) or the beneficiary.
- Interest Rate: 4% per annum (fixed concessional rate for the beneficiary).
- Repayment Period: The loan must be repaid in quarterly installments within 4 years. This includes a moratorium period of 6 months on recovery of the principal.
- Target Beneficiaries: Women entrepreneurs belonging to Backward Classes (as notified by Central or State Governments) living below or near the poverty line (annual family income limit is up to ₹3,00,000).
- Typical Trades: Tailoring, boutique, small grocery, dairy, beauty parlor, handicrafts.

2. MICRO CREDIT SCHEME (MCS) - NBCFDC
- Description: A micro-finance scheme implemented by the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (NBCFDC) to provide small, self-employment loans for individual projects or Self-Help Groups (SHGs).
- Loan Limits:
  * Maximum loan limit per individual beneficiary is ₹1,25,000 (the local matching portal supports up to ₹1,50,000).
  * Maximum loan limit per Self-Help Group (SHG) is ₹15,00,000.
- Pattern of Financing: NBCFDC provides loan assistance of up to 90% of the project cost. The channel partner or the beneficiary must contribute the remaining 10%.
- Interest Rate: 5% per annum (fixed rate for the beneficiary). Note: The local matching portal evaluates candidate schemes up to a 6% limit.
- Repayment Period: The loan must be repaid in quarterly installments within 4 years, which includes a principal moratorium period of 6 months.
- Target Beneficiaries: Micro-entrepreneurs (both male and female) belonging to Backward Classes (as notified by Central or State Governments) whose annual family income is up to ₹3,00,000.
- Typical Trades: Small shops, local trading, repair services, carpentry, weaving.

3. PM VISHWAKARMA SCHEME
- Description: A Central Sector Scheme launched by the Ministry of MSME to support traditional artisans and craftspeople who work with their hands and tools, providing them end-to-end support, recognition, and credit.
- Credit Support (Enterprise Development Loan):
  * Total Credit Limit: Up to ₹3,00,000.
  * The loan is collateral-free and provided in two separate tranches:
    - Tranche 1: Up to ₹1,00,000 with a repayment period of 18 months.
    - Tranche 2: Up to ₹2,00,000 with a repayment period of 30 months (available after fully repaying Tranche 1).
  * Concessional Interest Rate: A fixed rate of 5% per annum is charged to the beneficiary. The Government of India provides an interest subvention of up to 8% to the banks.
- Toolkit Incentive: A grant/incentive of ₹15,000 is provided via e-RUPI/e-vouchers for purchasing modern, improved tools after the artisan completes skill verification and starts basic training.
- Skill Training & Stipend:
  * Basic Training: 5 to 7 days of training with skill verification.
  * Advanced Training: 15 days or more of advanced skill development.
  * Daily Stipend: Beneficiaries receive a stipend of ₹500 per day during training, transferred directly to their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.
- Eligible Traditional Trades (18 Trades):
  1. Tailor (Darzi)
  2. Barber (Naayi)
  3. Welder
  4. Carpenter (Suthar/Badhai)
  5. Blacksmith (Lohar)
  6. Potter (Kumhaar)
  7. Cobbler (Charmakar/Shoemaker)
  8. Mason (Rajmistri)
  9. Basket/Mat/Broom Maker & Coir Weaver
  10. Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional)
  11. Sculptor (Murtiakar, Stone carver) / Stone Breaker
  12. Goldsmith (Sunar)
  13. Locksmith
  14. Armourer
  15. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
  16. Boat Maker
  17. Fishing Net Maker
  18. Garland maker (Malakaar)
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * An artisan or craftsperson working in one of the 18 family-based traditional trades.
  * Minimum age of 18 years on the date of registration.
  * Restricted to one member per family (family defined as husband, wife, and unmarried children).
  * Persons in government service and their family members are not eligible.
  * Registration is conducted through Common Service Centres (CSCs) using Aadhaar authentication.

4. PRIME MINISTER'S EMPLOYMENT GENERATION PROGRAMME (PMEGP)
- Description: A credit-linked subsidy programme administered by the Ministry of MSME, with KVIC acting as the national-level nodal agency, designed to establish new micro-enterprises in manufacturing, services, or trading.
- Project Cost Ceilings (New Projects):
  * Manufacturing Sector: Maximum project cost is ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs).
  * Service or Business/Trading Sector: Maximum project cost is ₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakhs).
- Margin Money Subsidy Rates & Contributions:
  * General Category:
    - Urban areas: 15% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 10% of the project cost.
    - Rural areas: 25% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 10% of the project cost.
  * Special Category (includes SC, ST, OBC, Women, Minorities, Ex-servicemen, Physically Handicapped, NER, Hill/Border areas):
    - Urban areas: 25% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 5% of the project cost.
    - Rural areas: 35% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 5% of the project cost.
- Loan Details: The funding banks (public, cooperative, or rural banks) sanction a term loan for the remaining balance. Interest is charged at normal commercial bank lending rates.
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * Any individual above 18 years of age.
  * Educational Qualification: Minimum 8th standard pass is mandatory for manufacturing projects costing above ₹10,00,000 and service/trading projects costing above ₹5,00,000.
  * Restrictions: Only new enterprises/projects are eligible for the PMEGP subsidy. Existing units or units that have already availed of government subsidies under state or central schemes are not eligible.
  * There is no household income ceiling to apply for this assistance.
  * Applications must be submitted online via the official PMEGP e-Portal managed by KVIC.
