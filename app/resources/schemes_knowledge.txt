OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SCHEME POLICY GUIDELINES & FAQS (SOURCE OF TRUTH)
================================================================================

1. MAHILA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA (MSY) - NBCFDC
- Description: A micro-finance scheme implemented by the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (NBCFDC) to provide credit assistance to women entrepreneurs belonging to Backward Classes to start or expand small businesses.
- Loan Limits:
  * Maximum loan limit per individual beneficiary is ₹1,25,000 (the local matching portal supports up to ₹1,40,000).
  * Maximum loan limit per Self-Help Group (SHG) is ₹15,00,000.
- Pattern of Financing: NBCFDC provides loan assistance of up to 95% of the project cost. The remaining 5% is contributed by the State Channelizing Agency (SCA) or the beneficiary.
- Interest Rate: 4% per annum (fixed concessional rate for the beneficiary).
- Repayment Period: The loan must be repaid in quarterly installments within 4 years. This includes a moratorium period of 6 months on recovery of the principal.
- Target Beneficiaries: Women entrepreneurs belonging to Backward Classes (as notified by Central or State Governments) living below or near the poverty line (annual family income limit is up to ₹3,00,000).
- Typical Trades: Tailoring, boutique, small grocery, dairy, beauty parlor, handicrafts.

2. MICRO CREDIT SCHEME (MCS) - NBCFDC
- Description: A micro-finance scheme implemented by the National Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation (NBCFDC) to provide small, self-employment loans for individual projects or Self-Help Groups (SHGs).
- Loan Limits:
  * Maximum loan limit per individual beneficiary is ₹1,25,000 (the local matching portal supports up to ₹1,50,000).
  * Maximum loan limit per Self-Help Group (SHG) is ₹15,00,000.
- Pattern of Financing: NBCFDC provides loan assistance of up to 90% of the project cost. The channel partner or the beneficiary must contribute the remaining 10%.
- Interest Rate: 5% per annum (fixed rate for the beneficiary). Note: The local matching portal evaluates candidate schemes up to a 6% limit.
- Repayment Period: The loan must be repaid in quarterly installments within 4 years, which includes a principal moratorium period of 6 months.
- Target Beneficiaries: Micro-entrepreneurs (both male and female) belonging to Backward Classes (as notified by Central or State Governments) whose annual family income is up to ₹3,0,000.
- Typical Trades: Small shops, local trading, repair services, carpentry, weaving.

3. PM VISHWAKARMA SCHEME
- Description: A Central Sector Scheme launched by the Ministry of MSME to support traditional artisans and craftspeople who work with their hands and tools, providing them end-to-end support, recognition, and credit.
- Credit Support (Enterprise Development Loan):
  * Total Credit Limit: Up to ₹3,00,000.
  * The loan is collateral-free and provided in two separate tranches:
    - Tranche 1: Up to ₹1,00,000 with a repayment period of 18 months.
    - Tranche 2: Up to ₹2,00,000 with a repayment period of 30 months (available after fully repaying Tranche 1).
  * Concessional Interest Rate: A fixed rate of 5% per annum is charged to the beneficiary. The Government of India provides an interest subvention of up to 8% to the banks.
- Toolkit Incentive: A grant/incentive of ₹15,000 is provided via e-RUPI/e-vouchers for purchasing modern, improved tools after the artisan completes skill verification and starts basic training.
- Skill Training & Stipend:
  * Basic Training: 5 to 7 days of training with skill verification.
  * Advanced Training: 15 days or more of advanced skill development.
  * Daily Stipend: Beneficiaries receive a stipend of ₹500 per day during training, transferred directly to their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.
- Eligible Traditional Trades (18 Trades):
  1. Tailor (Darzi)
  2. Barber (Naayi)
  3. Welder
  4. Carpenter (Suthar/Badhai)
  5. Blacksmith (Lohar)
  6. Potter (Kumhaar)
  7. Cobbler (Charmakar/Shoemaker)
  8. Mason (Rajmistri)
  9. Basket/Mat/Broom Maker & Coir Weaver
  10. Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional)
  11. Sculptor (Murtiakar, Stone carver) / Stone Breaker
  12. Goldsmith (Sunar)
  13. Locksmith
  14. Armourer
  15. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
  16. Boat Maker
  17. Fishing Net Maker
  18. Garland maker (Malakaar)
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * An artisan or craftsperson working in one of the 18 family-based traditional trades.
  * Minimum age of 18 years on the date of registration.
  * Restricted to one member per family (family defined as husband, wife, and unmarried children).
  * Persons in government service and their family members are not eligible.
  * Registration is conducted through Common Service Centres (CSCs) using Aadhaar authentication.

4. PRIME MINISTER'S EMPLOYMENT GENERATION PROGRAMME (PMEGP)
- Description: A credit-linked subsidy programme administered by the Ministry of MSME, with KVIC acting as the national-level nodal agency, designed to establish new micro-enterprises in manufacturing, services, or trading.
- Project Cost Ceilings (New Projects):
  * Manufacturing Sector: Maximum project cost is ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs).
  * Service or Business/Trading Sector: Maximum project cost is ₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakhs).
- Margin Money Subsidy Rates & Contributions:
  * General Category:
    - Urban areas: 15% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 10% of the project cost.
    - Rural areas: 25% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 10% of the project cost.
  * Special Category (includes SC, ST, OBC, Women, Minorities, Ex-servicemen, Physically Handicapped, NER, Hill/Border areas):
    - Urban areas: 25% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 5% of the project cost.
    - Rural areas: 35% subsidy. Beneficiary contribution is 5% of the project cost.
- Loan Details: The funding banks (public, cooperative, or rural banks) sanction a term loan for the remaining balance. Interest is charged at normal commercial bank lending rates.
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * Any individual above 18 years of age.
  * Educational Qualification: Minimum 8th standard pass is mandatory for manufacturing projects costing above ₹10,00,000 and service/trading projects costing above ₹5,00,000.
  * Restrictions: Only new enterprises/projects are eligible for the PMEGP subsidy. Existing units or units that have already availed of government subsidies under state or central schemes are not eligible.
  * There is no household income ceiling to apply for this assistance.
  * Applications must be submitted online via the official PMEGP e-Portal managed by KVIC.

5. PRADHAN MANTRI MUDRA YOJANA (PMMY)
- Description: A flagship scheme launched to provide collateral-free micro-loans to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises engaged in manufacturing, trading, services, and allied agricultural activities.
- Loan Categories & Limits:
  * Shishu: Loans up to ₹50,000 (for start-ups and initial stage ventures).
  * Kishor: Loans from ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000 (for mid-stage enterprises seeking expansion).
  * Tarun: Loans from ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 (for established small businesses requiring larger capital).
  * Tarun Plus: Loans from ₹10,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 (available for successful Tarun borrowers seeking further scaling).
- Interest Rate: Variable rates. Set individually by the lending institutions (banks, NBFCs, MFIs) based on credit risk profile and RBI guidelines.
- Collateral: No collateral or third-party guarantee is required.
- Repayment Period: Typically between 3 to 5 years, as determined by the loan sanctioning bank.

6. STAND-UP INDIA SCHEME
- Description: Central government scheme designed specifically to promote entrepreneurship at the grassroots level among women and Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities.
- Loan Limits: Composite loans (term loan and working capital) between ₹10,00,000 and ₹1,00,00,000 (₹10 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore).
- Eligible Projects: Setup of greenfield enterprises (first-time business ventures) in manufacturing, services, trading, or agricultural allied activities.
- Shareholding Constraint: In case of non-individual enterprises, at least 51% of the shareholding and controlling stake must be held by an SC, ST, or woman entrepreneur.
- Funding Structure: The loan covers up to 85% of the project cost. The beneficiary contribution is a minimum of 10% (margin money requirement is capped at 15%).
- Repayment Period: Repayable in up to 7 years with a maximum moratorium period of 18 months.
- Interest Rate: Linked to the bank's base lending rate (MCLR) plus a concessional premium.

7. PM STREET VENDOR'S ATMANIRBHAR NIDHI (PM SVANIDHI)
- Description: A special micro-credit facility scheme to provide affordable working capital loans to street vendors to resume their livelihoods post-pandemic.
- Loan Limits (Tranches):
  * 1st Tranche: Up to ₹15,000 (repayable within a 12-month tenure).
  * 2nd Tranche: Up to ₹25,000 (repayable within an 18-month tenure, available after timely repayment of the 1st tranche).
  * 3rd Tranche: Up to ₹50,000 (repayable within a 36-month tenure, available after timely repayment of the 2nd tranche).
- Interest Subsidy: Eligible for an interest subsidy of 7% per annum. The subsidy is credited directly to the borrower's bank account on a quarterly basis (contingent on keeping the loan account standard).
- Collateral: Completely collateral-free working capital loan.
- Prepayment Penalty: No prepayment charges or penalties for early repayment.
- Digital Cashback: Monthly cashback incentives up to ₹100 for vendors performing digital transactions.

8. MICRO CREDIT FINANCE (MCF) - NSFDC
- Description: A micro-finance scheme implemented by the National Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation (NSFDC) under the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment to provide small loans to individuals and Self-Help Groups (SHGs) from Scheduled Caste communities for income-generating activities.
- Loan Limits:
  * NSFDC finances up to 90% of the project cost, subject to a maximum of ₹1,25,000 to ₹1,40,000 per beneficiary.
  * Maximum funding per Self-Help Group (SHG) is up to ₹15,00,000.
- Interest Rate: Concessional interest rate of 6.5% per annum for the ultimate beneficiary (charged through State Channelizing Agencies / MFIs).
- Repayment Period: Repayable in quarterly installments within 3 years from disbursement, including a 3-month moratorium.
- Target Beneficiaries: Individuals belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC) communities with annual family income up to ₹3,00,000 (both rural and urban). For SHGs, all members must satisfy eligibility criteria.
- Typical Trades: Small grocery/kirana, tailoring, betel leaf shop, bakery, candle making, cycle repair, goat rearing, beauty parlor, vegetable vending, agarbatti making, tea stall, and micro-enterprises.

9. NHFDC TERM LOAN SCHEME (DIVYANGJAN SELF-EMPLOYMENT)
- Description: A concessional credit scheme administered by the National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation (NHFDC), Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, providing financial assistance to Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan) to establish self-employment ventures.
- Loan Limits: Concessional loans up to ₹50,00,000 per beneficiary depending on project viability and category.
- Pattern of Financing: Covers up to 90% of the project cost; the remaining 10% is contributed by the State Channelizing Agency and/or the beneficiary.
- Interest Rate: Concessional rates between 5% and 9% per annum based on loan slab (lower rates for loans up to ₹5,00,000). A special 0.5%–1.0% interest rebate is provided to women with disabilities.
- Repayment Period: Typically up to 3 to 7 years depending on project scale, with an initial moratorium period.
- Target Beneficiaries: Indian citizens with 40% or more disability (certified under the RPwD Act, 2016) aged 18 years and above.
- Associated Sub-Schemes: Also provides educational loans (up to ₹10 Lakhs in India / ₹20 Lakhs abroad) and assistive device loans.

10. UDYOGINI SCHEME (WOMEN ENTREPRENEURS)
- Description: A dedicated self-employment loan and subsidy scheme specifically designed for women entrepreneurs across rural and semi-urban areas to start micro-enterprises in trade, service, and manufacturing.
- Loan Limits: Loans ranging from ₹1,00,000 up to ₹3,00,000 based on project type.
- Government Subsidy: Up to 30% government subsidy on the project cost for general category women, and up to 50% subsidy (or interest-free terms) for SC/ST women, widows, destitute women, and women with disabilities.
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * Applicant must be a woman entrepreneur aged between 18 and 55 years.
  * Annual family income ceiling is typically up to ₹1,50,000–₹2,00,000 (waived or relaxed for widows and destitute applicants).
  * No prior default history on loans with any bank or financial institution.
- Typical Trades: Covers 88+ small business categories including tailoring, food catering/canteen, grocery shops, handicrafts, beauty clinics, and dairy products.

11. NMDFC TERM LOAN & MICRO FINANCE SCHEMES (MINORITY COMMUNITIES)
- Description: Concessional credit schemes implemented by the National Minorities Development & Finance Corporation (NMDFC), Ministry of Minority Affairs, for self-employment and micro-enterprise development among notified minority communities (Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis, Jains).
- Term Loan Scheme:
  * Credit Line-1: Loans up to ₹20,00,000 at 6% interest p.a. for applicants with annual family income up to ₹98,000 (rural) / ₹1,20,000 (urban).
  * Credit Line-2: Loans up to ₹30,00,000 at 8% interest p.a. (6% for female beneficiaries) for applicants with annual family income up to ₹8,00,000.
- Micro Credit / SHG Finance: Loans up to ₹1,00,000–₹1,50,000 per SHG member and up to ₹30,00,000 per SHG of women beneficiaries.
- Artisan Support (Virasat Scheme): Concessional working capital and tool/machinery financing up to ₹10,00,000 at 5% p.a. interest for traditional minority craftspersons.
- Target Beneficiaries: Individuals and SHGs belonging to the six notified National Minority communities satisfying the respective income criteria.

12. EDUCATION LOAN SCHEME (ELAS) - APEX CORPORATIONS (NBCFDC / NSFDC / NSTFDC / NHFDC)
- Description: Concessional education loan scheme channelized through State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) and Public Sector Banks to support students from marginalized communities pursuing professional or technical education.
- Loan Limits:
  * Studies in India: Up to ₹10,00,000 (₹10 Lakhs).
  * Studies Abroad: Up to ₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakhs).
- Pattern of Financing: Up to 90% of total education cost (covering tuition fees, books, examination fees, and boarding/lodging).
- Interest Rate: Highly subsidized rate of 4% per annum (with a 0.5% interest rebate for female students, effectively 3.5% p.a.).
- Repayment & Moratorium: Repayment tenure up to 5 years after the moratorium period. Moratorium duration is the entire course duration PLUS 6 months (or getting a job, whichever is earlier).
- Channel Partners: Channelized through State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs), Public Sector Banks (PSBs), and Regional Rural Banks (RRBs).
- Target Beneficiaries: Students from OBC, SC, ST, or PwD categories with annual family income below ₹3,00,000 enrolled in recognized higher education courses.

13. GENERAL TERM LOAN SCHEME - APEX CORPORATIONS (NBCFDC / NSFDC / NSTFDC)
- Description: Medium-to-large project credit assistance for setting up viable self-employment ventures in manufacturing, services, trading, or agriculture-allied sectors.
- Loan Limits: Up to ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs) per beneficiary or project unit.
- Pattern of Financing: Up to 85%–90% of the project cost is provided by the Apex Corporation. The remaining 10%–15% is met through SCA subsidy or promoter contribution (margin money).
- Interest Rate: Concessional lending rate between 6% and 8% per annum charged to the beneficiary.
- Repayment Period: Up to 5 to 10 years repayable in quarterly installments, including a moratorium period of 6 to 12 months on the principal repayment.
- Channel Partners: State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs), Public Sector Banks (PSBs), and Regional Rural Banks (RRBs).
- Target Beneficiaries: Marginalized entrepreneurs (SC, ST, OBC, Minorities, Divyangjan) satisfying annual family income criteria.

14. ADIVASI MAHILA SASHAKTIKARAN YOJANA (AMSY) - NSTFDC
- Description: Exclusive concessional micro-credit scheme by the National Scheduled Tribes Finance and Development Corporation (NSTFDC), Ministry of Tribal Affairs, for Scheduled Tribe (ST) women entrepreneurs.
- Loan Limits: Concessional term loan for income-generating activities with a project cost up to ₹2,00,000 per beneficiary.
- Pattern of Financing: NSTFDC finances up to 90% of the project cost.
- Interest Rate: 4% per annum for the beneficiary.
- Repayment Period: Repayable in quarterly installments within 5 to 10 years, including a suitable moratorium period.
- Channel Partners: Implemented through State Scheduled Tribes Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) and RRBs.
- Target Beneficiaries: Women belonging exclusively to Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities.

15. SWACHHTA UDYAMI YOJANA (SUY) - NSKFDC
- Description: Concessional credit and capital subsidy scheme by the National Safai Karamcharis Finance & Development Corporation (NSKFDC), Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, for sanitation enterprises and equipment.
- Loan Limits: Up to ₹15,00,000 for individuals/SHGs (sanitation vehicles and mechanized cleaning machinery) and up to ₹25,00,000 for community toilets.
- Capital Subsidy: Up to 50% upfront capital subsidy (capped at ₹3,25,000) for identified manual scavengers and dependents under rehabilitation.
- Interest Rate: 4% to 6% per annum (1% rebate for women).
- Repayment Period: Up to 7 to 10 years with a 6-month moratorium.
- Channel Partners: State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs), PSBs, and RRBs.
- Target Beneficiaries: Safai Karamcharis, manual scavengers, and sanitation workers.
